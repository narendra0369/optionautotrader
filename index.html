Methodology: signal confirmed at bar close; entry at the next bar open; stop-first assumption if TP and SL both touched; costs deducted; no leverage or position-size assumptions; equity normalized to R units. Exhaustive scans are computationally intensive, particularly on mobile. Candidate selection is based only on training data; the last 30% of candles remain unseen until validation.
